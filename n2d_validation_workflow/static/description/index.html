Sales Validation Workflow

Enhance your sales process with a validation step

Key features:
  • Add a validation step to your sales workflow
  • Enforce review before quotations can be sent to customers
  • Control user access with validation permissions
  • Lock quotations from changes after validation

Module Description

This module will be applied on sales to check the validation of the quotation before sending it or confirming it to be a sales order.

Workflow Steps

1. The user or admin of sales will make the quotation


2. Then the sales validation manager will click on the validate button


3. After clicking on the validate button, the quotation status will convert from quotation to validated


4. In this step if the quotation is in the validated status, the quotation will be locked, it can't be updated


5. After the quotation is validated, three buttons will appear: (reset to draft), (send by email) and (confirm)



  • Reset to draft: Enables the admin of sales to convert the state of the quotation from validated back to draft or quotation
  • Send by email: Enables the user or admin of sales to send the quotation to the customer
  • Confirm: Enables only the admin of sales to convert the quotation to a sales order

Important Notes

1. The validation button appears only for users who have sales validation manager rights


2. The reset to draft button and the confirm button appear only to the administrator in sales

Benefits

  • Improved quality control for customer quotations
  • Clear separation of responsibilities between sales users and approvers
  • Audit trail of validation actions
  • Prevention of unauthorized changes to approved quotations
  • Streamlined approval process with visual workflow
